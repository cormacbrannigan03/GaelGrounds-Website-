Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy explains what information GaelGrounds ("we", "us") collects when you use the GaelGrounds app, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. GaelGrounds is operated from Ireland, and this policy is written with GDPR in mind.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app:

  • Account information — the email address and password you sign up with. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form; we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Profile information — your display name and the county you choose to support.
  • Activity data — the matches you check into, the grounds you log as visited, any matches you add manually, and the timestamps of that activity.
  • Social data — friend requests and connections you make with other users, if you're a Premium subscriber.
  • Content you upload — photos you choose to add to a ground's photo gallery.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active Premium subscription and when it renews. This is provided to us by Apple after you purchase through the App Store — we never receive or store your card details.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the app's core features — check-ins, your personal record, achievements, friends and the leaderboard.
  • Some information is visible to other users by design: check-ins at a match, ground photos, your display name, and — if you're a Premium subscriber — your position on the leaderboard.
  • To communicate with you about your account when necessary (e.g. a support request you send us).

3. Who we share it with

  • Supabase, our database and infrastructure provider, which stores app data on our behalf, hosted in the EU.
  • Apple, which processes your Premium subscription payment. We never receive your payment card details — only confirmation of your subscription status.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use advertising trackers in the app.

4. Where your data is stored

App data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the EU (Ireland region).

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, or ask us to, we'll delete your personal data, other than where we're required to keep records for a longer period by law.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to how it's processed. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

GaelGrounds is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at hello@gaelgrounds.ie.
