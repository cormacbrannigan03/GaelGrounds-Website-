3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Contract: account creation, check-ins, match logs, core app functionality — necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest: leaderboard, friend features, achievements — reasonably expected as part of a social/gamified app, and you can opt out by not using those features.
  • Consent: any optional marketing communications (if introduced later) — will use a separate, specific opt-in.

4. Who we share data with

  • Supabase (our backend/database provider) — processes all app data on our behalf under a Data Processing Agreement. Supabase's servers/subprocessors are covered by their own DPA and Standard Contractual Clauses for any data transferred outside the EEA.
  • Apple — handles App Store account, download, and payment data as an independent controller under Apple's own privacy policy, not ours.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We do not currently use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs; if that changes, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and app data: kept for as long as your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, or ask us to delete it: your data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Deleted data may persist in routine backups for up to 30 days after deletion, for disaster-recovery purposes only, before being permanently purged.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data (data portability)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these, email cormac.brannigan03@gmail.com. We'll respond within one month, as required by GDPR.

You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) if you think we've mishandled your data.

7. Premium subscription (once launched)

If you subscribe to GaelGrounds Premium (€1.99/month), billing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status — never your payment details. You can cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings. See our Terms of Service for the 14-day withdrawal/cooling-off right.

8. Children

GaelGrounds is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and, where required, notify you in-app.
