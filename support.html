Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. Or email us and we'll do it for you — deleted within 30 days either way.

How do I cancel Premium?

Through your Apple ID subscription settings (Settings app → your name → Subscriptions), or the cancel link inside the app. Apple manages all billing — we never see your card details.

I found incorrect ground or match data — how do I report it?

Email us with the details. Historical scores are compiled from public sources and corrections are always welcome.
